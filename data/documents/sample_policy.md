# Company Policies

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least 48 hours in advance.

### Eligibility
- All full-time employees who have completed their probationary period (90 days)
- Role must be compatible with remote work (determined by department head)
- Employee must have reliable internet connection and appropriate workspace

### Requirements
- Must be available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Must attend all required meetings via video call
- Must respond to messages within 2 hours during working hours

## Expense Policy

### Travel Expenses
- Flights must be booked economy class for trips under 6 hours
- Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $200/night in major cities
- Meals are reimbursed up to $75/day

### Equipment
- Employees receive a $500 annual stipend for home office equipment
- All equipment purchases over $100 require manager approval
- Company laptops must be returned upon termination

## Time Off Policy

### Paid Time Off (PTO)
- All employees receive 15 days PTO per year
- PTO accrues at 1.25 days per month
- Maximum PTO balance is 25 days (use it or lose it)

### Holidays
The company observes 10 paid holidays per year:
1. New Year's Day
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day
3. Presidents Day
4. Memorial Day
5. Independence Day
6. Labor Day
7. Thanksgiving Day
8. Day after Thanksgiving
9. Christmas Eve
10. Christmas Day

## IT Security Policy

### Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days
- Cannot reuse last 5 passwords

### Data Handling
- Confidential documents must not be stored on personal devices
- All company data must be accessed through VPN when off-site
- Report any suspected security incidents to security@company.com immediately
